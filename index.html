Forecast quality

WAPE = total absolute prediction error / total actual demand. Chronological holdout: days 75–89. Lower is better. Forecasts predict amounts, not fraud risk or creditworthiness.

What this does not prove

  • No real customer or upay transaction data was used.
  • More accurate forecasts do not guarantee better business economics.
  • Visit costs are assumed at ৳150 per completed visit.
  • Cash snapshots and attempted demand may not be observable in production.
  • The test generator resembles training conditions; shock scenarios expose only some limitations.
INSPECTABLE BY DESIGN

A small model.
A complete decision loop.

Every amount has a source. Every action has a constraint. Every result can be replayed.

01

Synthetic demand

90 days, 12 agents, 10 operating hours per day. Separate cash-in and cash-out streams. Three agent archetypes, daily patterns, weekends and scheduled paydays.

Request-level replay includes attempted and failed requests, not only successful transactions.

02

Learned forecasting

Ridge regression learns log hourly amounts from 14 transparent features. Validation selects regularization. A training-only correction maps log predictions back to expected amounts.

Days 0–59 train; 60–74 validate; 75–89 test. Surprise shocks are never model inputs.

03

Constrained planning

A greedy heuristic uses predicted cumulative imbalance to propose cash/e-float exchanges. It respects exchange caps, visit capacity, finite hub inventory and a chosen lead time.

No optimality claim. A stale balance stops recommendations. A delivery that becomes infeasible is skipped.

04

Human review & replay

The operator approves a batch. The simulator rechecks every delivery, applies matched exchanges and processes customer requests in time order.

Changing a scenario or constraint resets approval. No live financial action is available.

Responsible use & integration

This public demo contains synthetic identities and balances only. A production version would need authenticated roles, reliable balance reconciliation, approved transaction/request feeds and provider-authorized settlement. The local approval UI is not production authorization.

Forecast, planning and replay are separate modules. There is no free-form LLM in the decision path, no secret API key, no payment integration and no claim of official upay affiliation.

Future validation: governed historical evaluation → shadow mode → controlled operator-reviewed pilot. Access is conditional.
